## 1 · The dispatch problem（35 秒）

**标题**：A batch goes outside on one chat message, unchecked and unrecorded

**左栏 TODAY**
- 灰色气泡：“@Chen — order 042, 1,500 crewnecks. Send it to QuickStitch, need it back by the 19th.”
- 三个编号问题：
  1. **Can they make it?** Not every workshop makes every product.
  2. **Can they take it now?** A fast shop with a 3-day queue is not fast today.
  3. **Are they allowed to?** One shop is suspended after an audit; one is on trial, small batches only.
- 斜体一行：Get one wrong and the batch bounces back a day later. In a ten-day cycle that is the margin gone.
- 加粗一行（决策问题）：**The decision: which eligible workshop, with capacity, returns the batch by its due date — and why.**

**右上 WHAT THE DESK MUST DO**（范围，三类能力，六项小字对应老师原文）
- **Read** the chat: extract the request; ask when it is ambiguous or incomplete; refuse, with a reason and an alternative, when the named workshop cannot take it; honour constraints and record who set them.
- **Decide** on numbers: keep the workshop register; estimate a delivery date per candidate before committing; allocate with the reasoning attached; when nobody can make the date, say so and lay out the options.
- **Record**: an auditable record of every decision — what the group chat never produced.
- 小字：Optional, assessed after Sprint 2: the stage extension (`--stages`). Not pursued: multi-agent negotiation (the mechanism is not graded).

**右下 BUSINESS VALUE**（只讲方向，不放数字）
- Dispatcher: faster decisions with verified recommendations
- Factory: fewer late deliveries, shown in simulation
- Customer: more reliable delivery-date commitments

**讲稿**：Today the decision is a chat message. Behind it are three questions; get one wrong and the batch comes back. So the desk has to read the chat, decide on numbers, and leave a record. The value is a verified recommendation for the dispatcher, fewer late batches for the factory, and a date the customer can rely on.

## 2 · Solution and architecture（50 秒）

**标题**：The language model reads; the code decides; the dispatcher confirms

**上半：五格流程图**（颜色：蓝 = 语言模型，深色 = 代码，橙 = 人）
1. **Chat message** (INPUT) — free text from five people
2. **Language layer** (LLM + CHECK) — the model extracts order, pieces, date, constraint
3. **Kernel** (CODE, NO LLM) — register, estimator, allocator; the objective is a setting
4. **Explain and check** (CODE) — writes the reason from tool outputs
5. **Dispatcher** (HUMAN) — confirms, changes or escalates; then the audit record is written

**图上三个醒目标记**（技术机制，这页的重点）
- ① **Text decides.** Every value the model extracts is checked against the message; anything the message does not contain is dropped.
- ② **Eligibility before ranking.** Three hard checks first; then every eligible shop gets two dates — expected, and if the batch is reworked — and the ranking follows the chosen objective.
- ③ **Traced and confirmed.** Every number in the explanation must come from a tool output; capacity is booked and the audit record written only after the dispatcher confirms.

**下半：R12 走一遍**（一行五格）
- Message: “ORD-053 — 800 polo shirts for UrbanThread, due Apr 10. Boss says nothing new to QuickStitch this week.”
- Language layer: ORD-053 · 800 pcs · due 10 Apr · constraint: QuickStitch excluded, standing, set by Boss
- Kernel: 4 eligible (QuickStitch excluded, OldMill suspended, FreshStart over its 300 cap, Little Loom accessories only) · GiantWeave back Fri 10 Apr, Sat 11 Apr if reworked
- Explain: “GiantWeave: on time; it only makes tops, so the two shops that also make accessories stay free for tighter batches.”
- Dispatcher: confirms → ledger updated, audit record A0004

**小字**：Graded by the shared simulator (harness/simulate.py v3): 120 orders replayed against three baselines; the shock option closes one workshop for two weeks.

**讲稿**：The model only reads. Code checks every extracted value against the text, runs three hard eligibility checks, gives each eligible shop two dates, and ranks by the objective. The explanation is written from tool outputs, and nothing is booked until the dispatcher confirms. Here is one real message going through: the standing constraint is recorded, four shops are eligible, GiantWeave makes the date even if reworked.

## 3 · Why minimise late deliveries（40 秒）

**标题**：Primary objective: minimise late deliveries

**标题下定义行**
- Late-delivery rate = batches returned after their due date ÷ all batches. We also report mean days late (0 for an on-time batch). Both are the simulator's own metrics.

**左栏 WHY THIS ONE**（三层论证）
1. **Relevant.** The due date is the one constraint every order carries; quick response exists to protect it — “the factory that farms out a batch today beats the one that decides on Thursday.”
2. **Measurable.** The simulator records when every batch returns, so lateness is computed, not estimated.
3. **Controllable.** Lateness depends on queue, capacity and transport — exactly what the choice of workshop changes.
- 小字补充：The three baselines are 34–92% late in simulation: the largest gap, but supporting evidence, not the reason.

**右栏 THE OTHER THREE**（老师要求的其他目标和混合）
- Defects and fairness: switchable settings of the same allocator; results reported for the same 120 orders.
- Hybrid: one weighted setting (late 1.0, finish 0.3, defect 0.25, load 0.5), weights chosen on held-out seeds.
- Robust to failure: tested with the shock scenario; our guardrail is lateness no worse than random.

**底部一行（宣布护栏）**【主策略】
- 若定新规则：Declared tie-break: when two settings are equally late, we take the shorter turnaround — the track names turnaround days as the business impact.
- 若定 use_slack：Primary policy: the slack-keeping setting; turnaround reported as its cost.

**讲稿**：We minimise late deliveries because the due date is the one constraint every order carries, the simulator measures it directly, and the choice of workshop is exactly what moves it. Defects and fairness stay as switchable settings, plus one hybrid; robustness is tested with the shock scenario.

## 5 · Risks, assumptions and limitations（45 秒）

**标题**：What could go wrong, and what we are assuming

**表格：三大风险**

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| No clean language test yet: the 30 official messages were used in development; the 38 new ones are AI-written | We may overstate how well the model reads | Held-out inbox of 44 messages written by teammates who do not touch the prompt; answers frozen before the first run (Sprint 2) |
| The simulator differs from reality: seven-day week; the estimator does not know when a workshop closes | Lateness looks better than it would be | Sunday variant reported separately (simple rule 1.7 → 3.7%, ours → 2.1%); closed-workshop detection in Sprint 2 |
| Our online policy is not globally optimal: a hindsight search that knows every order in advance reaches 0% late in the tested setting | Room we have not captured | A dispatcher cannot see future orders; we report the gap and keep the rule simple enough to explain |

**假设一行小字**：Requests arrive in time order and a confirmed dispatch occupies capacity · a standing constraint (“nothing new to QuickStitch this week”) applies to every later request · a reworked batch takes half as long again (simulator rule) · the data holds no customer prices and no history, so those questions are declined.

**讲稿**：Three risks. We have no clean language test yet, so teammates who never touch the prompt will write a held-out inbox. The simulator is kinder than reality; we report a Sunday variant and will add closed-workshop detection. And our policy is not optimal: a hindsight search reaches zero, but a dispatcher cannot see the future. Our assumptions are on the bottom line.

## 问答预案（评审列的五问）

1. **Why lateness over defects or fairness?** → 第 3 页三层论证；补一句：defects and fairness are reported as settings on the same orders.
2. **You tie the simple rule; what is your contribution?** → On fresh order books we are ahead (72 of 100 sets); on official data we halve the late days; and the desk adds what no allocator has: reading the chat, refusing, two dates, the audit record.
3. **Why not the 1.0% policy?** → Paired runs: its edge holds only on the official orders (8 of 10 seeds); on 200 fresh sets the two are indistinguishable on lateness; the robust difference is 5 days of turnaround and 9% cost. The track names turnaround as the business impact, so we declared the faster tie-break.
4. **How do you know the LLM generalises?** → We do not yet; that is risk 1. The 38 AI-written messages are a smoke test (97–100%); the held-out inbox is the real test.
5. **What if a workshop shuts down?** → Today: the shock run, 2.0% late, the system does not notice. Sprint 2: detect the stuck queue and reroute; acceptance criterion on slide 6.
